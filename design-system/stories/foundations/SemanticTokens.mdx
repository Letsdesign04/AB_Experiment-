# Semantic colour tokens

These are the colours components use. Each one names a **job** (text, background, border, icon, focus, chart) and points at a primitive. Click any variable to copy it.

Contrast is measured against the surface each token sits on: white (`--bg-primary`) by default, brand blue for `on-brand`, and dark for `on-dark`. Disabled states are exempt under WCAG. Body text needs **4.5:1** (AA). Icons, borders and large text need **3:1**.
