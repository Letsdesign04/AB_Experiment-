## Usage guidelines

- Use `subtle` to separate closely related items; `default` for section breaks; `strong` for major layout boundaries.
- Labelled dividers work well in authentication flows ("Or sign in with").
- Vertical dividers suit inline navigation, breadcrumbs, and toolbar separators.
